# Beef steak with grilled vegetables and chimichurri sauce

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

## 操作

1. Place the oven on to preheat at 180 degrees.
2. -- for scaling
3. slice the beef cut (200g) into 200 g pieces.
4. Slice the courgette (50g) and aubergine (50g) into slices a little less than a centimetre thick so that they cook evenly, cut red bell peppers (50g) into large chunks.
5. Finely chop the fresh coriander (50g) and parsley (50g), then deseed and finely chop the red chilli (10g), red onion (20g) and garlic (2g) clove.
6. Mix the herbs, chilli and garlic with the red onion and peppers in a bowl, add salt (0.5tsp), sugar (1tsp), vinegar (10g) and freshly ground black pepper (0.33tsp), mix thoroughly so that the acid dissolves the sugar and salt, into this mixture add a little olive oil, enough to create the desired consistency.
7. Brush the vegetables lightly with olive oil and using a grill pan, heat over a high heat and roast the courgettes, aubergines and bell peppers.
8. Then line a baking tray with tinfoil, place the courgettes on the foil and roast for 12 minutes in the oven.
9. Season the beef steak with salt and pepper on both sides, brush with cooking oil and grill in a frying pan over a medium heat for 2 minutes on each side until seared and golden brown.
10. After frying, place in the oven on the tray with the vegetables. Cook for 6-10 minutes, depending on how you want the steak done. The longer you cook the meat, the more well done the steak will be. When the meat is cooked to your liking, remove the steak and vegetables, place on a plate and drizzle over the chimichurri sauce and serve extra sauce on the side, garnishing with a sprinkling of extra chopped herbs.
