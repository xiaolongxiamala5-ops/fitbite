# Prawn and avocado salad

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- avocado
- king prawns
- thyme
- rosemary
- garlic
- oil
- salt
- rocket salad leaves
- lime
- olive oil

## 计算

- avocado = 0.5
- king prawns = 100 g
- thyme = 0.2 tsp
- rosemary = 0.2 tsp
- garlic = 1 glove
- oil = 适量
- salt = 适量
- rocket salad leaves = 20 g
- lime = 30 g
- olive oil 适量

## 操作

1. Cut the avocado (0.5) in half, remove the stone and carefully peel off the skin. Cut the avocado into slices. A little lemon juice will prevent it going brown if necessary.
2. Peel the king prawns (100g), heat a frying pan over a medium heat with a little oil and sauté the prawns with the salt, thyme (0.2tsp), rosemary (0.2tsp) and chopped garlic (1glove). Should you like chilli you can finely chop one chilli and add to the prawns.
3. Arrange the avocado on a plate, top with the rocket salad leaves (20g), place the prawns on the rocket salad and drizzle with lime (30g) juice and olive oil. This salad makes a lovely, light Summer lunch.
