# Greek salad

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- tomatoes
- cucumber
- red bell pepper
- lettuce
- pesto
- olives
- red onion
- feta cheese

## 计算

- tomatoes = 50 g
- cucumber = 50 g
- red bell pepper = 50 g
- lettuce = 40 g
- pesto = 20 g
- olives = 20 g
- red onion = 10 g
- feta cheese = 50 g

## 操作

1. Wash tomatoes (50g), cucumber (50g), red bell pepper (50g) and cut into large chunks, tear up the lettuce (40g) and mix together in a bowl with the other ingredients.
2. Add the pesto (20g) and olives (20g), toss lightly. Place on a plate, garnish with thinnly sliced rings of red onion (10g) and blocks of feta cheese (50g). If necessary drizzle with a little olive oil and season.
