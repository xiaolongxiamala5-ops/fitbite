# Pesto sauce

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- Parmesan cheese
- pine nuts
- garlic
- parsley
- basil leaves
- salt
- pepper
- olive oil

## 计算

- Parmesan cheese = 20 g
- pine nuts = 10 g
- garlic = 1 g
- parsley = 50 g
- basil leaves = 85 g
- salt = 2 g
- pepper = 0.25 tsp
- olive oil = 140 g

## 操作

1. -- Pesto
2. Grate the Parmesan cheese (20g) on a fine grater.
3. Roast the pine nuts (10g) in a dry pan until they become shiny as the oils realease and you just begin to smell them.
4. Place the garlic (1g), parsley (50g) and basil leaves (85g) in the blender bowl and season with salt (2g) and pepper (0.25tsp), then blend the ingredients together in the blender to create a paste.
5. Dilute the mixture with olive oil (140g) and lemon juice to get the consistency you want. Pesto is fantastic with pasta, salads or just on ciabatta bread.
