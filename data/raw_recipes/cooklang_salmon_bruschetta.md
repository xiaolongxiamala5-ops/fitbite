# Salmon bruschetta

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- ciabatta
- oil
- cottage cheese
- smoked salmon
- capers
- dill
- black pepper
- lemon

## 计算

- ciabatta = 50 g
- oil = 适量
- cottage cheese = 50 g
- smoked salmon = 80 g
- capers = 5 g
- dill = 1 g
- black pepper 适量
- lemon = 适量

## 操作

1. Slice the whole ciabatta (50g) loaf in half and then slice the halves lengthwise. Fry the ciabatta halves in a frying pan in a little oil, a griddle pan gives the best results and makes nice lines as the bread toasts.
2. Spread the cottage cheese (50g) on the toasted ciabatta liberally, cut out the smoked salmon (80g) slices and place on the bread with a sprinkling of capers (5g) and finally chopped dill (1g).
3. Serve with a lemon wedge and freshly cracked black pepper.
