# Cabbage bake with mushroom cream

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- cabbage
- mushroom soup powder
- cream

## 计算

- cabbage = 0.5
- mushroom soup powder = 1 packet
- cream = 250 ml

## 操作

1. -- This is a lovely, simple recipe that goes with just about anything and is a real crowd pleaser.
2. Preheat the oven to 180 degrees Celsius.
3. Slice your cabbage (0.5), it can be roughly done as the cabbage will cook down in the oven. Place in an oven proof dish and sprinkle the mushroom soup powder (1packet) over the top. Pour over the cream (250ml) and place in the oven.
4. Keep a check on the cabbage, it will need a stir once in a while. Your dish should be done in about 35 minutes. No seasoning is really necessary as the soup powder is normally quite well seasoned.
