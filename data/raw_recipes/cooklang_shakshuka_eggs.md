# Shakshuka eggs

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- shakshuka sauce
- eggs
- chiabatta

## 计算

- shakshuka sauce = 100 g
- eggs = 2
- chiabatta = 30 g

## 操作

1. -- TODO include sauce properly
2. Prepare the shakshuka sauce (100g) first from ./Shakshuka sauce.cook.
3. Place the sauce in a frying pan and heat it up slowly, beat eggs (2) gently.Cook the eggs in a pan over a low heat until light and fluffy.
4. Serve with warmed or toasted chiabatta (30g) bread.
