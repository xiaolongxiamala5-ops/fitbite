# Shakshuka sauce

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- onions
- red bell peppers
- salt
- cumin ground
- smoked paprika
- sugar
- chopped tomatoes

## 计算

- onions = 200 g
- red bell peppers = 400 g
- salt = 1 tsp
- cumin ground = 0.2 tsp
- smoked paprika = 0.2 tsp
- sugar = 1 tsp
- chopped tomatoes = 1 tin

## 操作

1. Peel the onions (200g) and finely slice them, core and deseed the red bell peppers (400g), slice them into thin julienne slices.
2. Heat a frying pan and sauté the vegetables in a little oil until softened, then add the salt (1tsp), cumin ground (0.2tsp), smoked paprika (0.2tsp), sugar (1tsp) and chopped tomatoes (1tin). Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer to reduce slightly then taste for seasoning and remove from the heat. Serve with eggs and ciabatta bread.

## 附加内容

- 本菜谱来源于 Cooklang 官方开源精选食谱库。
- 遵循健康轻食、控油控糖原则烹饪。
