# Turkey fillet in tomato souce

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- jasmin rice
- white quinoa
- salt
- turkey breast fillets
- chicken breast fillets
- frozen corn
- frozen green beans
- red onion
- red bell peppers
- oil
- tinned tomatoes
- sugar

## 计算

- jasmin rice = 50 g
- white quinoa = 30 g
- salt = 2 g
- turkey breast fillets = 125 g
- chicken breast fillets = 125 g
- frozen corn = 40 g
- frozen green beans = 40 g
- red onion = 40 g
- red bell peppers = 40 g
- oil = 1 tbsp
- tinned tomatoes = 40 g
- sugar = 5 g

## 操作

1. Rinse the jasmin rice (50g) and white quinoa (30g) under running water to get rid of the starch. Pour the water into a saucepan, add the rice and quinoa, season with salt (2g) and simmer on a medium heat.
2. Rinse and pat dry the turkey breast fillets (125g)[- | chicken breast fillets (125g) -] gently with paper towel. Cut them into cubes.
3. Defrost the frozen corn (40g) and frozen green beans (40g) and cut into slices. Often these are already prepared for you.
4. Peel red onion (40g) and finely chop along with red bell peppers (40g).
5. Heat a frying pan with a little oil (1tbsp) and sauté the onion and pepper for 5 minutes. Add the turkey or chicken fillets to the vegetables, stir-fry for about 5 minutes and add the green beans and corn, stir-fry for another 5 minutes.
6. The quinoa rice should be cooked by this time, just check to make sure it is al dente'. Remove side dish from the heat. Add the tinned tomatoes (40g) to the turkey, add sugar (5g) to balance acidity and salt (0.5tsp) to taste. Braise for 5 minutes. Place the quinoa rice on a plate and top with the turkey/ chicken and vegetables. Garnish with chopped parsley.
