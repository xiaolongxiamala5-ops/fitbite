# Minestrone with homemade chicken stock

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- chicken broth
- carrots
- courgette
- bell peppers
- vegetable oil
- frozen green beans
- cherry tomatoes
- garlic
- basil leaves

## 计算

- chicken broth = 400 g
- carrots = 20 g
- courgette = 20 g
- bell peppers = 20 g
- vegetable oil 适量
- frozen green beans = 15 g
- cherry tomatoes = 20 g
- garlic = 1 clove
- basil leaves = 5 g

## 操作

1. -- TODO should really refer servings
2. Prepare the chicken broth (400g) first from the ./Chicken broth.cook recipe.
3. Wash and peel the carrots (20g), wash the courgette (20g) and bell peppers (20g) and cut into small cubes.
4. Heat a pan with vegetable oil over a medium heat, sauté the chopped vegetables together with the frozen green beans (15g) until soft, add the cherry tomatoes (20g) and chopped garlic (1clove) then remove from the heat.
5. Heat the chicken stock in a saucepan, add the softened vegetables and bring to the boil, once boiling remove from the heat and pour into a bowl. Garnish with finely chopped parsley or basil leaves (5g). This is a wonderful Winter warmer.
