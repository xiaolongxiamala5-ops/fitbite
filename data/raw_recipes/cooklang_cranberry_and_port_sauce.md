# Cranberry and port sauce

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- port
- brown sugar
- cranberry jelly

## 计算

- port = 300 ml
- brown sugar = 175 g
- cranberry jelly = 2 tbsp

## 操作

1. Place the port (300ml) and brown sugar (175g) into a pot and allow to boil.
2. Add the cranberry jelly (2tbsp) and gently simmer so that the sauce can reduce, make sure to keep stirring your sauce to stop it catching on the bottom of the pan. The longer you simmer the sauce, the thicker it will get.
3. This is quite a sweet gravy and so goes well with poultry and potatoes.
