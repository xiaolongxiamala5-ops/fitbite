# Smoothie bowl

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- strawberries
- banana
- blueberries
- milk
- oat flakes
- chia seeds
- granola
- salt
- fresh blueberries
- fresh raspberries

## 计算

- strawberries = 50 g
- banana = 1
- blueberries = 50 g
- milk = 100 g
- oat flakes = 30 g
- chia seeds = 10 g
- granola = 10 g
- salt = 1 g
- fresh blueberries = 10 g
- fresh raspberries = 10 g

## 操作

1. -- TODO add options for fresh or frozen
2. Blend strawberries (50g), banana (1), blueberries (50g), milk (100g), oat flakes (30g), chia seeds (10g), granola (10g), salt (1g) with a blender, allow to settle for 10 minutes, pour into a bowl and garnish with fresh blueberries (10g) and fresh raspberries (10g). You can use fresh or frozen fruit for this smoothie.
