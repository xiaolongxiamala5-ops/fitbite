# Light chicken soup with buckwheat noodles

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- chicken broth
- eggs
- soba noodles
- green pepper
- chicken stock
- chicken fillets
- soy sauce

## 计算

- chicken broth = 400 g
- eggs = 1
- soba noodles = 40 g
- green pepper = 5 g
- chicken stock = 0.4 l
- chicken fillets = 50 g
- soy sauce 适量

## 操作

1. Make up the chicken broth (400g) first from the ./Chicken broth.cook recipe.
2. Put a sauté pot{} on the stove and boil the eggs (1), then boil the soba noodles (40g), following the instructions on the back of the package.
3. The carrots for the broth can be cut into chunks and boiled till soft. Cut the green pepper (5g) into small rings.
4. Put the casserole on the stove, pour chicken stock (0.4l) into the pot and bring to the boil, cut the chicken fillets (50g) into strips and boil in the stock for about 5 minutes, then add the chopped boiled carrots and boiled soba noodles to the stock.
5. Pour the broth into a soup bowl, peel the boiled egg and slice in half, place into the soup, add the soy sauce to taste and garnish with green peppers.
