# Beef tar-tar

Cooklang 官方开源减脂轻食菜谱，注重优质蛋白与时蔬搭配，制作流程清晰明了。

预估烹饪难度：★★
预计制作时间：20 分钟

## 必备原料和工具

- beef loin
- red onion
- salt
- pepper
- Worcester sauce
- ciabatta

## 计算

- beef loin = 120 g
- red onion = 15 g
- salt = 0.25 tsp
- pepper = 0.2 tsp
- Worcester sauce = 15 g
- ciabatta = 2 slices

## 操作

1. Remove any excess fat or sinew and finely dice the beef loin (120g) or beef fillet.
2. Peel and finely dice the red onion (15g).
3. Mix together the diced beef and onion in a mixing bowl, season with salt (0.25tsp), pepper (0.2tsp) and Worcester sauce (15g). You can also add some finely chopped parsley to the tar-tar.
4. Toast the ciabatta (2slices) in a pan without oil. It's nice to use a griddle pan to get the toasted lines.
5. Place the tar-tar on a plate and serve with the ciabatta and a wedge of lemon.
